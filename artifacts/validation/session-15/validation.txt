  NOT CloudWatch custom metrics: billed per metric/month, ~20 series x 12 datasets.

  The trap it introduces, and the fix:
    Pushed metrics are STICKY. A job that pushes freshness=3min and never runs again
    leaves Prometheus reporting 3 minutes FOREVER — pipeline dead, dashboard green.
    Every push emits cdc_lakehouse_pushed_timestamp_seconds, and PipelineMetricsStale
    keys off THAT. It is the only signal separating 'healthy' from 'gone'.
